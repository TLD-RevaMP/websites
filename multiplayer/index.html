Playing The Long Drive multiplayer

The beta branch of The Long Drive has a multiplayer mode. TLD RevaMP replaces it with its own, where everyone can drive anywhere on the map. Nobody has to stay near the host. It runs over Steam, so you don't need port forwarding, Hamachi or Radmin VPN.

Everyone needs the game on the beta branch with the same RevaMP version installed. The game refuses a version mismatch and says so.

Hosting

  1. Load or start a world.
  2. Press F11, or open Multiplayer in the pause menu, and press Host. A password is optional.
  3. Your Steam friends now see your game in their Multiplayer menu.

The world and the save stay on the host's PC.

Joining

  1. Press F11, or open Multiplayer from the main menu.
  2. Click a friend who is hosting, then Join. Type the password if the game has one.

Your game loads the host's world. Joining never touches your own saves.

While playing

The multiplayer menu lists the players and their ping, and has Mute, and Kick for the host. Leave or Stop hosting is in the same menu.

Text chat opens with Enter and closes with Escape. For voice, hold the game's Voice chat key (Settings → Controls; K if it isn't bound). Players within 60 m hear you. It uses the microphone you picked in Steam (Steam → Settings → Voice). Push to talk, open mic, off and voice volume are in Settings → Revamp.

What's shared between players

  • Players on foot, seated, driving or riding along, with their footsteps and sounds. You can stand in a moving bus or on a car.
  • Cars: driving, crashes, damage, parts coming off, fuel and fluids.
  • Loose items you carry, throw or drop.
  • Doors and gates of buildings.
  • Creatures: hunting, attacks, shots, deaths and bodies.
  • Guns, bullet holes and explosions.
  • Sandstorms, tumbleweeds and the radio.

Each player's game runs the cars, items and creatures near that player. That's why you can split up across the map and things still move for everyone.

If something goes wrong

Press Page Down right when it happens. That saves a report zip with a screenshot and logs in BepInEx\tldrevamp-feedback in the game folder. Then tell us what happened on GitHub.

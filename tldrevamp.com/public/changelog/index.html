Changelog

What changed in each version of TLD RevaMP. Everyone in a session needs the same version. Each heading links to that release on GitHub, with its full notes and download.

0.69.2 20 Nov 2026

  • Releases are now built for players: no debug settings or developer keys. Report a bug with Ctrl+Insert or /bug in chat. The performance overlay starts hidden; Page Up shows it.
  • A Mods button on the main menu and in the pause menu opens the mod list, with search and sorting, to install, update, disable, enable or remove mods. At start, RevaMP checks for mod updates and asks before installing them.
  • If your multiplayer mods differ from the host's, a Required mods screen installs the host's versions from the mod list. Hosts no longer send mod files. A joiner whose game version, game files, TLD Loader mods or world-generation mods differ from the host's is refused with a message.
  • Joining shows a loading screen and holds you in place until the ground and the objects around you are there.
  • Hosts get Session settings: teleport requests, where joiners appear, voice chat, password and developer commands. Joiners' Dev Mode is off unless the host allows it.
  • Many multiplayer fixes: doors, mirrors and knobs move smoothly for everyone, other players hear your starter and see you steer, trailers hitch to another player's car without flinging it, and dropped items land in the same spot on every screen.
  • New games: choose Crossroads, Main road only or Old terrain (2023) on the new-game screen. The old terrain needs extra files, and the screen says where to get them.
  • Replays (Ctrl+F9 records, Ctrl+F10 plays) and a filming camera with fly and orbit modes. The camera has no key until you set one.
  • VR: the controllers work, with hands that grab, throw and use things, your real height, and comfort settings.
  • Anti-aliasing is MSAA plus a filter (FXAA, SMAA, TAA or FSR 3.1 native AA), MSAA 2x + SMAA by default. New shadow, reflection and performance options, and detailed terrain reaches 750 m by default.
  • Settings → Revamp → Keys changes RevaMP's keys in game. With the game's Dev Mode on, F1 still opens its menu; its other hotkeys do nothing until you set them under Settings → Revamp → Dev Mode keys.
  • Everyone in a session must update: 0.69.1 and 0.69.2 can't join each other.

0.69.1 17 Oct 2026, dev build

  • In-game mod browser: Settings → Revamp → Mods installs and removes approved mods. Changes apply after a restart.
  • Install scripts for Windows (install.ps1) and Linux/Steam Deck (install.sh): install, update, repair and uninstall. They check what they download and back up files they replace.
  • All of the mod's code, including the multiplayer API, is GPL-3.0.

0.69.0 17 Oct 2026

  • The host sees which players left, what waits for them and where, and can forget a player.
  • Admin commands in chat: /players, /kick, /ban, /say, /save, /password, /maxplayers and more. Bans last across sessions.
  • Dedicated servers take commands from a text file and answer in another.
  • The host checks shots, blows and explosions on cars, creatures and items, as it already did for players.
  • Multiplayer API part 2 for mod makers, and a scanner that shows what a TLD Loader mod would need for multiplayer.
  • Rabbits 200–300 m away no longer write a warning every frame.
  • A car resting on a slope stays where its owner has it on everyone's screen.

0.68.5 17 Oct 2026

  • A hat you wore when you left is back on your head when you return, and the other players see it there too.

0.68.4 17 Oct 2026

  • Sandstorms are much rarer, most pass by in the distance, and each dies down after 15–30 minutes.
  • New setting Settings → Revamp → World → Sandstorms: Off, Rare, Normal or Often. In multiplayer the host's setting counts.

0.68.3 17 Oct 2026

  • Players get their pockets back after the host restarts the game.
  • Pockets are saved with the save they belong to, so items no longer double or vanish after loading an older save.
  • The Car01's radiator breaks when its fuel tank explodes, also in single player.
  • The teleport-request setting is remembered.

0.68.2 17 Oct 2026

  • Hats, caps, glasses, helmets and masks sit on the player wearing them, for everyone, also in moving cars.
  • What you wear comes back with you when you rejoin.
  • Something taken out of a car or off a pallet shows where it was put.
  • A part destroyed in an explosion is gone for everyone.
  • The host refuses players joining under someone else's Steam ID, and limits what one player can change or send.

0.68.1 16 Oct 2026

  • Items in a car go with it when someone else takes the wheel, instead of flinging the car.
  • Cars that went far away come back with all their parts. Older saves are repaired when they load.
  • A building missing for one player is named in the host's log.

0.68.0 15 Oct 2026

  • Rabbits are back, and everyone sees the same ones, including their attacks and bites.
  • Trailers brake with the car towing them.
  • Optional driving assists: trailer sway reduction, roll correction and traction control.

0.67.2 15 Oct 2026

  • The IFA drives straight: its four driven wheels no longer fight each other.
  • The game's own trailer no longer pulls the tow car's hitch down.
  • A towed trailer and the car on it no longer jump when the tow car sets off.
  • An unhitched trailer no longer rolls away on a slope.

0.67.1 15 Oct 2026

  • The host is told when a player moves farther or faster than possible.
  • Dedicated servers get a settings section: port, password, player limit, teleport requests and autosave interval.
  • The question to download the host's mods no longer answers itself when you join from inside a world.

0.67.0 15 Oct 2026

  • Leave a friend's world and come back as you left: same place, same things, food, water and health.
  • Teleport requests: /tpa <name>, answered with /y or /n. Your car and its passengers come along.
  • Everyone plays by the host's game mode (survival, peaceful enemies, biome, homesick).
  • First multiplayer API for other mods.
  • Trailers can be hitched again, and a loaded trailer can be towed by another player.
  • Cars on a transporter or trailer stay on.

0.66.9 14 Oct 2026

  • The host checks every hit on a player against where everyone was at that moment, so a modified game can't hurt players from across the map.
  • Joiners can no longer save the host's world over their own.

0.66.8 14 Oct 2026

  • Items stored in a car no longer wreck it when taken out.
  • Bolted-on parts stay on a friend's car after a crash far away.
  • A car no longer vanishes and comes back every second.
  • Radio dials turn as a passenger at speed.
  • Everyone is told when someone joins with a different game version.

0.66.7 9 Oct 2026

  • Another player's car no longer vanishes and comes back.
  • Cars appear where they really are, with all their parts, after being out of view.
  • Crate loot is the same for everyone.
  • Radiator, oil and fuel caps stay as you set them.

0.66.1 8 Oct 2026

  • Stand in the back of a driving bus, or on a truck bed, and ride along.
  • Other players walk smoothly.
  • Joiners appear next to the host.
  • Now shared: car doors, hood and trunk, lights, paint, crouching, food, the radio and bus back seats.
  • New keys that don't clash with the game's: F11 multiplayer menu, Page Up performance overlay, Page Down bug report. Push to talk is the game's Voice chat key, or K.

0.65.3 2 Oct 2026

  • New multiplayer menu, passwords and kick.
  • Text chat, and proximity voice chat up to about 60 m.
  • Sandstorms, tumbleweeds and player sounds are back and shared.

0.64.11 to 0.64.13 2 Oct 2026

  • Pushing another player's car or objects works.
  • Radio stations play the same song for everyone.
  • Other players' horns, brake lights and lamps show.
  • Bodies fall and lie the same for everyone.
  • Walking another player's vehicle by its grip works.

0.64.3 to 0.64.7 1–2 Oct 2026

  • New worlds no longer leave buildings empty.
  • Wheels of other players' cars sit at the right height.
  • Items at nearby buildings reach everyone.
  • A bus spawned by one player arrives with all its wheels.
